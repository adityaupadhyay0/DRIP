# Contributing to DRIP

Thank you for your interest in DRIP! We welcome contributions from the community.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork the repository**.
2. **Create a new branch** for your feature or bug fix.
3. **Make your changes** and ensure they follow our coding standards.
4. **Submit a pull request** with a clear description of your changes.

## Development Setup

See the [README](README.md) for instructions on setting up the project locally.

## Community

Join our community to discuss features and get help.

## Code of Conduct

Please be respectful and inclusive in all your interactions within the project.
